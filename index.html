How to play?
  1. 👀 A grid of pictures shows for a few seconds. Remember where each one is!
  2. 🙈 Then the pictures hide.
  3. ✋ Put every picture back in its box before the timer runs out. Tap a picture, then tap its box.
  4. ⭐ Every picture in the right box is 1 point. Each wrong one costs you that point: 9 − 1 = 8.
  5. 📈 3 rounds: 3×3, then 4×4, then 5×5. Most points after 3 rounds wins!
